3B
Rank the six groups
Which of these six matters most for the people you hire, and which matters least? Use each number once.
Group 1: Conceptual Understanding of Artificial Intelligence Systems
Understanding how these systems actually work: how they are built from data, the different types, how they fail, and where AI is already sitting inside services you use.
Group 2: Critical Evaluation of AI Outputs and System Performance
Judging what comes out of them: is it accurate, is it biased, does the reasoning hold up, is this image real, is that claim about AI credible or is it marketing.
Group 3: Ethical and Social Implications of Artificial Intelligence
The ethical and social side: personal data, energy cost, the effect on jobs, keeping a human in charge of decisions, and knowing when not to use AI at all.
Group 4: Practical Application and Professional Use of Artificial Intelligence Tools
Practical use: prompting, choosing tools, using AI without letting it do your thinking for you, and fixing it when it goes wrong.
Group 5: Governance, Accountability, and Regulation of Artificial Intelligence
Governance and accountability: is a tool trustworthy, do you declare where you used AI, the law, professional codes, intellectual property, and who carries the can.
Group 6: Cognitive Diversity, Accessibility, and Inclusive AI Literacy
Inclusive use: adapting how AI is used across different languages and different ways of thinking, and checking whether the tools themselves are fair.
